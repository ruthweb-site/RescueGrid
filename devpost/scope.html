MISSION BRIEF: Deterministic risk scoring and supply triage paired with auditable, hallucination-proof AI action directives.
SIMULATED DATA ONLY
The Unique Kernel

The deterministic, auditable boundary between math and AI: risk scoring and resource allocation are computed strictly via transparent, rule-based algorithms, while the AI only synthesizes, explains, and drafts human-readable emergency directives.

The AI's narrative plan is validated against the computed numbers so it cannot hallucinate or invent inventory. If validation fails or the API times out, an automated template-based fallback plan is seamlessly displayed—guaranteeing the plan never contradicts the numbers.
